# Fanout full public context

> A citation-oriented map of Fanout's canonical public pages. It contains descriptions and discovery links, not protected lesson bodies or private user data.

## Identity and scope

Fanout is an independent technical learning product created by Suraj Gaud. It organizes AI research, system design, machine learning mathematics, inference engineering, open robotics, practical browser-local labs, and daily paper notes. Fanout distinguishes its own summaries and teaching material from third-party papers, books, courses, repositories, and community profiles; outbound sources remain the canonical sources for those works.

## Access and interpretation

- Public route descriptions and Daily paper notes may be summarized and cited with their Fanout URLs.

- Pro lesson and article bodies are served only after entitlement checks and are not reproduced here.
- Login, account, checkout, redirect, error, and protected detail routes are excluded from this public context.
